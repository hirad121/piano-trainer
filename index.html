Keyboard mapping (GarageBand-style - home row = white keys, no mouse needed): A S D F G H J K L ; ' = white keys, W E T Y U O P = black keys, Z / X (or the ← / → arrow keys) = shift the whole mapping down / up an octave. Falling notes are colored blue for left hand, orange for right hand. In Practice-wait and Play mode, releasing a key flashes it green for a well-timed hold, amber for too short, red for too long. Pressing a key that isn't currently due flashes it red as wrong; in Play mode, a note whose moment passes unplayed also flashes red as a miss. Play mode is the real test - notes don't wait for you - and clearing a song there at 90%+ good (at 1x speed or faster) unlocks the next one.
